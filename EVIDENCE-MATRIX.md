## Notes on language discipline

- "Field validated" always means: a post-fix capture (or sustained multi-session field record) shows
  the specific class absent under conditions that previously produced it.
- Where a fix shipped on source inference + corroboration (E17), the matrix says so and withholds
  validation until the reproduction passes.
- Bucket attributions marked *inferred* derive from hash-ring matching with known ±40-tick ambiguity
  (see `KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md`).
